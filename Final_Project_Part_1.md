# Final Project – Part 1: Language Design & Grammar

- **Due:** Sunday by 11:59pm
- **Points:** 100
- **Weight within Final Project:** 10%
- **Submission:** One PDF or Word document per group

---

## Objective

In Part 1, your group will design the programming language that you will implement throughout the remaining five parts. Your goal is to establish the syntax, basic language features, and grammar before beginning implementation.

---

## Requirements

Your language must eventually support:

- Variable declaration and assignment
- Numeric values
- Arithmetic operators: `+`, `-`, `*`, `/`
- Parentheses and operator precedence
- Comparison operators
- Print statements
- `if`/`else`
- `while` loops
- Simple functions with parameters and return values
- Basic variable scope
- **One additional language feature** chosen by your group

Choose a unique name for your programming language.

*Example syntax:*

```text
let x = 10;
let y = 20;
if (x < y) {
    print(x + y);
}
```
*(You may design a different syntax.)*

---

## BNF/EBNF Grammar

Develop an initial BNF or EBNF grammar describing your language.

Your grammar should define at least:

- `<program>`
- `<statement>`
- `<variable-declaration>` and `<assignment>`
- `<block>`
- `<expression>`, including comparisons and function calls
- `<term>`
- `<factor>`
- `<print-statement>`
- `<if-statement>`
- `<while-statement>`
- `<function-definition>`
- `<return-statement>`
